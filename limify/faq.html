Frequently asked questions

Answers about setting up purchase limits and stock rules with Limify.

General

What is Limify?

Limify is a Shopify app that lets you set product purchase limits, order quantity rules and stock-based limits to control how customers buy and to protect your inventory.

When can I install Limify?

Limify is coming soon to the Shopify App Store. Contact us if you'd like to hear when it launches.

How much does it cost?

Plans and prices will be shown on the Shopify App Store listing at launch.

Which Shopify plans does Limify support?

Limify is being built for all Shopify plans, including Basic, Shopify, Advanced and Shopify Plus.

Features and limits

What types of limits can I set?
  • Product purchase limits: a maximum quantity per product or variant
  • Order quantity limits: a minimum and maximum per order
  • Stock threshold rules: limits that change with inventory levels
Can I set limits per product or per variant?

Yes. Limits can be set for a whole product or for individual variants.

Do limits apply per customer or per order?

You can apply them per order, per customer, or both.

Can I set different limits for different products?

Yes. Each product or variant can have its own limit, and you can apply rules to whole collections or your entire catalog.

How do stock threshold rules work?

They adjust purchase limits based on current inventory. For example, you can lower the limit when stock drops below a set number to avoid overselling during busy periods.

Privacy and data

What data does Limify access?

Limify reads your store's products, orders and inventory through the Shopify API to enforce limits. We don't share this data with third parties. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Email support@appcellent.com at any time. When you uninstall the app, your store data is deleted within 30 days.

Troubleshooting

Limits aren't being enforced.
  • Check that the app is enabled in your Shopify admin.
  • Check that a limit is set for that product or variant.
  • Make sure the app block is active in your theme.
  • Test in a private browser window.
Customers can still go over the limit.
  • Check whether the limit is per order or per customer. Per-order limits can be bypassed with several separate orders.
  • Look for rules that conflict with each other.
  • Make sure the theme integration is installed.
