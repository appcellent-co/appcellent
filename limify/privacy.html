About this policy

Appcellent Yazılım Hizmetleri A.Ş. ("Appcellent", "we", "us") operates the Limify Shopify application ("the App"). This policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect information when you use the App. By installing and using the App, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Store information

  • Store details: your store name, URL and the configuration the App needs to work.
  • Product data: product names, variants, prices and inventory levels, used to set and enforce limits.
  • Order data: order quantities and customer identifiers, used to enforce per-customer and per-order limits.

Merchant information

  • Account information: the name and email linked to your Shopify account, used for support and service messages.

Collected automatically

  • Usage data: how you use the App in your Shopify admin, such as features used and settings changed.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the App.
  • To read product and inventory data and enforce your limits.
  • To apply per-order and per-customer rules.
  • To contact you about the App, including support and updates.
  • To find and fix technical problems.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

How we share information

We don't sell your information. We only share it:

  • With Shopify, because the App runs on Shopify's platform under its API terms and privacy policy.
  • When the law requires it, for example in response to a valid request from a public authority.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition.
  • With your consent, for any other purpose.

Retention

We keep your store and configuration data while the App is installed. After you uninstall, we delete your store data within 30 days. We may keep anonymous, aggregated usage statistics to improve the App.

Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information. All data sent between the App and Shopify is encrypted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Legal basis (EEA and UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process personal data because it is necessary to provide the App to you (contract), to improve and secure the App (legitimate interest), to comply with the law (legal obligation), or because you agreed to it (consent), which you can withdraw at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your personal data, object to its processing, or withdraw consent. To do so, email support@appcellent.com. If you are in the EEA or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The App is for Shopify merchants and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information, and we'll delete it if we learn we have.

International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, which may have different data protection laws. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will appear on this page with a new date at the top. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@appcellent.com.
