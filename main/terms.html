Measurements, estimates and AI

Several of our apps turn your phone's sensors or outside data into readings and estimates. Phones aren't professional instruments, and results depend on your device, its case, its surroundings and how it's used. Results are for general guidance only. In particular:

  • Stud Finder detects magnetic metal, such as screws and nails. It can't reliably detect wood, live electrical wires or pipes. Always confirm a location by other means, turn off power where needed, and take care before drilling, cutting or nailing.
  • Protractor and other measuring features give approximate values and shouldn't be used where precision matters for safety, construction or medical purposes.
  • Decibel Meter isn't a certified sound level meter and isn't suitable for legal, occupational or health compliance measurements.
  • Volume Booster can play sound at high levels. Listening at high volume for long periods can damage your hearing and your speakers. Use it at your own discretion.
  • TCG Vault shows estimated card values based on third-party market data. They aren't offers to buy or sell, can be out of date and may be wrong. Card identification may be inaccurate.
