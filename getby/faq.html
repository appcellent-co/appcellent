Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Getby.

Getting started

How do I add the delivery estimate to my store?
  1. Open Getby from your Shopify admin and create a default rule.
  2. Click Add to product page. Your theme editor opens with the "Delivery estimate" block already added.
  3. Click Save in the theme editor.
  4. Back in Getby, click Publish widget.

Repeat step 2 with Add to cart page to show estimates in the cart too.

Do I need to edit my theme code?

No. Getby installs as a theme app block through Shopify's theme editor. No code changes are made to your theme, and nothing is left behind if you uninstall.

Why don't I see the widget on my storefront?
  • Check that the widget is published in the Getby dashboard, not in draft mode.
  • Check that the "Delivery estimate" block is added and saved in your theme editor.
  • Check that at least one enabled rule matches the product. The default rule matches everything.

Delivery dates

How is the delivery date calculated?

Order date + processing time + shipping time, calculated in your shop's timezone. Orders placed after your cutoff count from the next working day. Non-working days and holidays are skipped for processing, and optionally for shipping too.

Can I show different delivery times for different products or countries?

Yes. Create rules by product, collection, tag or destination country. Rules are matched by priority, exclusions win over inclusions, and the default rule covers everything else.

What is the cutoff countdown?

If you set a cutoff time (for example 15:00), the widget shows a live countdown like "order within 2h 14m". The countdown always follows your shop's timezone.

Customization

Can I change how the widget looks?

Yes. Pick a ready-made preset or set your own colors, font and size. Four layouts are available: classic, minimal, timeline and badge.

Can I translate the widget text?

Yes. The text can be translated per language, with {date} and {countdown} placeholders you can place anywhere in your own wording.

Billing

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The free plan includes one store-wide delivery rule with cutoff, working days, holidays, and all widget templates and languages.

What does Pro include?

Pro is $9.99 per month or $49.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial. It adds unlimited rules, country and tag targeting, exclusions and priority support. Billing is handled by Shopify.

How do I cancel?

Cancel any time from the Plan page inside the app. Uninstalling the app also ends the subscription automatically.
