About this policy

Getby: Estimated Delivery Date ("Getby" or "the App") is operated by Appcellent Yazılım Hizmetleri A.Ş. ("Appcellent", "we", "us"). The App shows estimated delivery dates on Shopify storefronts. This policy explains what information the App processes and how it is handled.

Information we collect

When you install the App, we store your shop domain (for example your-store.myshopify.com), the API access token Shopify issues so the App can work, your shop's timezone, and the settings you configure in the App: delivery rules, cutoff times, working days, holidays, widget appearance and translations.

Your customers' data

The App does not collect, store or process personal data about your customers. To calculate an estimate, the storefront widget uses only non-personal context (product ID, product tags, collection IDs, the visitor's country code and language), and none of it is stored.

The App doesn't request any Shopify API access to customer or order data.

How we use information

Shop settings are used only to provide the App: calculating and showing delivery estimates on your storefront and letting you manage rules in the admin. We don't sell or share your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Data is stored on servers provided by Fly.io in the European Union (Frankfurt). All data in transit is encrypted with TLS.

Retention and deletion

When you uninstall the App, your access token and settings are deleted. We also honor Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks: when a shop redaction request is received, every record related to your shop is permanently removed. Because we store no customer data, customer data and redaction requests need no further action.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete the information we hold about your shop by emailing support@appcellent.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will appear on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@appcellent.com.
